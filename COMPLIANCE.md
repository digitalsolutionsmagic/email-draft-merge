# Before you send anything

A short checklist for cold email. This is general information, not legal advice. Rules differ by country, so check the law where you and your recipients are.

- Say who you are: your real name, your real business, and why you are writing.
- Keep the subject line honest. It should match what is in the email.
- United States (CAN-SPAM): the rules apply to business-to-business email too. Include a valid physical postal address and a clear way to opt out, and honor opt-out requests within 10 business days.
- EU and UK: consent and legitimate-interest rules are stricter, especially for individuals and sole traders. Check the rules before you email there.
- Keep a do-not-contact list, and check every new list against it.
- Only email addresses you have a real reason to use. Do not buy scraped lists.
- Start small, then watch bounces and complaints before you send more.

This tool only produces drafts. You are responsible for what you send.
